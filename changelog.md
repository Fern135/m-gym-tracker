# Changelog

Every commit adds an entry here, newest first.

## 1.0.1 — October 28, 2026

- The rest timer alarm now plays as a real alarm, so you hear it even when the phone is on vibrate or locked.
- New **Rest alarm sound** setting: pick Classic alarm, Digital watch, Rising siren or Ringing bell. Picking one plays a preview.
- The evening-before reminder now reads like a calendar event ("Upper Body", "Tomorrow at 8:00 AM").
- New **Evening-before sound** setting: pick Calendar chime, Soft marimba or Phone default.
- Two workouts can no longer have the same name (capitals and extra spaces don't count as different).
